🖥️ PC parts🏷️ Tech −30%+
    How are deals picked?
    • Tweakers: today's price drops across all Dutch shops.
    • Pepper: deals posted by the community at Dutch retailers, kept only when they're cheaper than every Dutch shop on Tweakers today.
    • Only PC parts that match your suggestion rules: RTX 3000+ / RX 5000+, otherwise at most 36 months old, no server parts.
    • Ranked by discount, with a bonus for real lows and a penalty for inflated "before" prices. At most 3 per category.
    • Collected every morning (and when you log in) by the scheduled task on your PC.
    Which tech deals are shown?
    • Everything on Tweakers Pricewatch (phones, laptops, TVs, audio, networking, PC parts…) that is at least 30% off today. The 10 biggest drops, at most 3 per category.
    • Only genuine lows: Tweakers' "lowest price in months", or at most 2% above the lowest price of the last 6 months.
    • No consumables, accessories or household appliances, nothing under €40, and no business or server equipment.

    🔑 GitHub connection

    Lets this page add and edit parts and start checks. Your token is stored only in this browser.

    How do I get a token? (1 minute)
    1. Open New fine-grained token on GitHub.
    2. Name it PC Price Tracker, pick an expiration (e.g. 1 year).
    3. Repository access → Only select repositories → .
    4. Permissions → Repository → Contents: Read and write and Actions: Read and write.
    5. Generate, copy, and paste it below.
